 If your source file is having 100 million records and only 2000 records are duplicated, how will you remove duplicates?
Using dropDuplicates([“customer_id”]) - optimal (1 shuffle and 1 scan)

Identify Duplicate Keys First :
Find duplicate customer_id
Finance all duplicate records  and non duplicate records
Use dropduplicate for only duplicate records 
Join the above step result to non duplicate records using unionByName 
 
Why is this better?
99,998,000 records remain untouched.
Only ~2,000 duplicate records undergo expensive deduplication logic.
Much lower shuffle and faster execution.
